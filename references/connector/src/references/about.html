References

Use References Add on a page to choose a backend and References project before saving. Captures stay bound to that destination.

References uses locally bundled translators and the authorized BashKitten backend. Publisher sign-in and human challenges open in ordinary tabs.

Capture privacy

Saving sends the page URL and referrer, reference metadata, notes and tags, and requested snapshots or PDFs to the backend and project you choose. The backend may be local or remote; it uses your existing authorized BashKitten connection.

Publisher retrieval uses your browser's website session. The capture extension has no analytics, developer error reporting or cloud-account fallback.
