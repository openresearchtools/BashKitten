# Appraise methods and evidence

Use for a study/result appraisal, checking existing judgements, or assessing a
body of evidence. Apply the [shared safeguards](../../SKILL.md). Appraisal is not
an automatic exclusion step or a generic quality score.

## Choose the right assessment

Establish the review question, design, and unit of judgement. Risk of bias may be
specific to an outcome, result, time point, or estimand rather than an entire
paper. Use the researcher's specified instrument when appropriate; discuss a
mismatch rather than silently substituting a different instrument.

Select design-appropriate guidance, for example RoB 2 for randomized trial
results, an applicable ROBINS-I version for non-randomized intervention evidence,
a diagnostic-accuracy tool, or a suitable qualitative appraisal framework.
Read the actual instrument and version being applied. Do not invent signaling
questions, scoring rules, or claim formal tool use from memory alone. If the
needed guidance is unavailable, offer a clearly labeled preliminary critique.

## Make judgements evidence-based

- Preserve domain/signaling-question judgements, source locators, supporting
  evidence, and rationale in whatever artifact is convenient. Distinguish an
  observed methodological problem from insufficient reporting or unavailable
  information, following the selected tool's rules.
- Consider design-relevant threats such as allocation/confounding, participant
  selection, deviations, missing data, measurement, selective reporting, and
  researcher reflexivity where applicable. Do not force all domains onto all
  study designs or penalize a design for an irrelevant criterion.
- Compare protocols, registrations, analysis plans, and companion reports when
  available and relevant. Record their dates/versions and access gaps. Do not
  infer prespecification from a post hoc assertion alone.
- Apply domain-to-overall decision rules transparently. Do not sum arbitrary
  points, average ordinal labels, or substitute prestige, reporting completeness,
  or statistical significance for risk of bias.
- Keep conflicting assessor judgements and reconciliation reasons. Flag decisions
  requiring methodological expertise or human adjudication; an agent appraisal
  is not independent human validation.

## Relate appraisal to synthesis

Study/result risk of bias, reporting quality, applicability, and certainty in a
body of evidence are different constructs. For certainty assessments, use an
appropriate framework and unit (such as an outcome or qualitative finding),
considering its prescribed domains and explanations. Do not automatically apply
one framework's rules to every type of review or convert sample size into certainty.

Follow prespecified uses of appraisal in synthesis; label new exclusions,
weighting schemes, or sensitivity analyses as amendments/exploratory choices.
Provide a reasoned account of how limitations could affect conclusions, not just
colored labels. Load [analysis](../analysis/SKILL.md) when testing robustness or
[writing](../writing/SKILL.md) when reporting these judgements.
