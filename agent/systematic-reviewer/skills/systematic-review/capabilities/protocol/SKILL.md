# Protocol and methods design

Use for a new question, a methods gap, or an amendment to an existing review.
Apply the [shared safeguards](../../SKILL.md). Do not restart an established
review or demand registration merely because this capability was loaded.

## Make methods answer the question

- Establish the intended audience, decision, scope, review type, and constraints.
  Choose a question framework only if useful: intervention, exposure, diagnostic,
  prognostic, qualitative, and scoping questions need different structures.
  Do not force every question into PICO or every review into a meta-analysis.
- Translate the question into operational eligibility criteria. Address relevant
  populations/context, concepts or interventions/comparators, study designs,
  outcomes/time frames, publication status, and language/date limits. Explain
  restrictions and their consequences rather than hiding convenience choices.
- Separate eligibility from retrieval feasibility, risk of bias, and synthesis
  grouping. For example, an eligible study may lack usable quantitative data.
  Decide how reports from the same study and overlapping populations will link.
- Align searches, screening, extraction, appraisal, and synthesis with those
  choices, at the level needed for the current task. Identify how uncertainty,
  missing information, disagreements, and unavailable full texts will be handled.
  Specify human review expectations; do not call repeated agent judgements
  independent reviewer assessment.
- Define outcomes, contrasts, units, time windows, and any selection rules before
  examining result direction when possible. Plan treatment of multiple measures,
  study reports, confounding, and dependencies if relevant. Prespecify meaningful
  sensitivity analyses; state when synthesis will remain narrative or thematic.

## Test the difficult boundaries

Try a few realistic edge cases against the proposed criteria: mixed populations,
partial exposure, mixed methods, missing outcome data, preprints, or follow-up
reports. Revise ambiguous rules with the researcher, not ad hoc while excluding
records. A small pilot should reveal unclear criteria, not guarantee validity.

Find the applicable reporting guidance or appraisal instrument version before
claiming compliance. A reporting checklist is not itself a research design or a
risk-of-bias tool. Registration, when applicable, is an explicit external action
requiring authorization; never imply it has occurred because a draft exists.

## Useful deliverable

Offer a readable methods note, edits to the existing protocol, or another agreed
artifact. Include unresolved choices and their trade-offs, not invented answers.
For amendments, retain the previous version, date, rationale, timing relative to
results, affected work, and whether re-screening or re-analysis is needed. Label
post hoc choices as such.

Load [harvesting](../harvesting/SKILL.md), [screening](../screening/SKILL.md), or
[analysis](../analysis/SKILL.md) only when implementing those particular choices.
No fixed columns or set of downstream artifacts is required.
